# Slack

Where the team talks. Everyone joins the workspace and the channels below on day one.

## Asana in Slack

Changes in our Asana project are posted to **#asana-tasks**, so nobody has to refresh the board to see what moved.

**What is posted** (project "Internship 2026 - Multi-currency Wallet"):

- a task is created
- the assignee of a task changes
- a task is completed
- a new comment is added

**What you need to do (once, everyone):**

1. Join **#asana-tasks**.
2. Link your own Asana account in Slack: open the direct message from the **Asana** app and click **Connect**
   (or run `/asana` and follow the prompt). Without it the app cannot show your name or task previews.

**Task link previews.** Paste an Asana task link in any channel and Slack shows a preview of the task
(name, assignee, status). Previews work only for people who have linked their Asana account.

**Good to know**

- The app can also list, create, comment on and complete tasks from Slack with `/asana`.
- Everyone in **#asana-tasks** sees the updates of the project, even if they are not a member of it.
  As always: no secrets or personal data in task titles and comments.
- Notifications are configured in Asana: project name → **Edit project settings** → **Notifications** → **Slack**.
